1. Executive Vision & Problem Statement

Standard NASA FIRMS implementations only visualize where heat anomalies are detected. However, FIRMS data alone cannot answer the critical operational question: "Is this heat an uncontrolled industrial disaster, a routine operational gas flare, a wildfire, or seasonal agricultural burning?"

The FlameX Paradigm Shift

FlameX transforms raw satellite heat coordinates into contextual, explainable intelligence by fusing satellite radiance (NASA FIRMS), infrastructure geofences (OpenStreetMap), 10m land cover (ESA WorldCover), and a 30-day temporal baseline engine.

2. The 5 Core Data Streams

Data Stream Source & Resolution Operational Role in FlameX
1. Thermal Radiance NASA FIRMS (VIIRS 375m / MODIS 1km) Ingests Fire Radiative Power (FRP in MW), brightness temperatures (T4/T5), detection confidence, and satellite timestamps.
2. Industrial GIS Context OpenStreetMap (OSM) Vectors Computes spatial proximity (distance in meters) to refineries, chemical SEZs, power plants, steel mills, and mines.
3. Land-Cover Context ESA WorldCover (10m Pixel Grid) Categorizes surface landuse: Industrial/Built-up, Dense Forest, Cropland, Mining/Bare Soil, Water.
4. Optical Verification Sentinel-2 MSI (10m Bands) Provides true-color (B04, B03, B02) and SWIR (B12) visual verification of smoke plumes and structural damage.
5. Population Exposure Demographic Population Grids Measures distance to nearest residential settlement zones to escalate hazard severity to CRITICAL.

3. How the AI Predicts: The Detective Methodology

When a heat anomaly is detected, FlameX executes a multi-feature decision algorithm answering three fundamental questions:

📍 1. WHERE is the Heat?

Checks underlying terrain via ESA WorldCover and distance to industrial assets via OSM. Anomaly within 500m of a refinery is tagged as industrial.

📈 2. WHAT is Normal for this Spot?

Queries the 30-day baseline engine. If a refinery flare burns at 108 MW every day (28/30 days), it is normal. If it surges to 380 MW (3.6× baseline) with no recurrence, it is an emergency fire.

👥 3. WHO is at Risk?

Evaluates human settlement proximity. If high radiance is within 1,000m of a populated town, FlameX escalates the incident to CRITICAL DISPATCH.
